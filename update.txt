UPDATE PROCEDURE

1. Update TSRG, CSV, mappings
2. Generate workspace and decompile
3. Inspect. Look for:
	a. Exceptions
	b. Access level issues
4. Using this data, write the exceptions.txt & access.txt files
5. Go back to step one. The MCInjector corrections will be applied.
6. Make a new workspace, inspect, and fix any other errors.
7. Generate patches.
8. Run the game. Maybe compare with an instance in Prism/BetaCraft or something.
9. Done!
